Final prompt:

Create ONE square premium fantasy game character portrait for Tsuventure,
matching reference images 1 (hamster knight) and 2 (rabbit mage) in sumptuous
detailed storybook cinematic fantasy illustration, rich textured materials,
shallow-depth-of-field enchanted ruins, magical bokeh and moonlight, NOT a
simple polygon render. Reference 3 is only the dragon identity to preserve:
adorable small jade mint-green dragon knight, round big head, gentle glossy
large dark eyes, rosy cheeks, two short cream horns, little blue-membrane wings,
cream belly, blue steel armor with delicate gold trim, small moon sword and
round blue-gold shield. Friendly brave expression, finely painted tiny scales.
Centered close-up chest-up portrait with face large and readable inside a
circular UI crop, head and upper armor fill the square like the hamster
reference; hints of wings/weapon/shield at edges. Painterly-realistic soft
fantasy craftsmanship matching hamster/rabbit. Background detailed but defocused
moonlit castle garden, warm golden fireflies and cool teal rimlight. No text,
logo, border, watermark. This is final in-game character art, opaque background.
